Each product has its own **Usage & billing** page — one for the AppSec Platform,
one for VibeDefend — where you pick a plan, adjust seats and projects, and follow
the current month's usage. The **Billing account** page in organization settings
holds what both products share: your billing identity, your payment method, your
invoices, and which products are active.

This page covers the AppSec Platform's plan and billing. For VibeDefend's monthly
allowances and spending caps, see [VibeDefend](/latest/agent-ai-integration/vibedefend#usage-and-monthly-limits).

## Subscription Details

Monitor and adjust your current subscription plan to match your organization's needs.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing/subscription-details.png" alt="Subscription Details" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

### Key Information

- **Plan Type**: View your current subscription plan (Trial, Pro, Enterprise, etc.)
- **Seats**: The seats your plan allows — the seats included in your plan plus any extra seats you have added
- **Trial Period**: If applicable, see when your trial ends
- **Billing Cycle**: View current period end and next reset date

### Seats and Invitations

Each plan comes with a number of seats, and an invitation is refused once they are
all taken. Members, open invitations and the invitation you are sending all count
against the total. Only people who hold the AppSec Platform take an AppSec seat —
see [Per-member product access](/latest/get-started/account-setup/managing-access-org-teams#per-member-product-access).

| Plan | Seats included | Seats maximum | Projects included | Projects maximum |
|------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Pro | 1 | 5 | 10 | 100 |
| Team | 5 | 25 | 30 | 250 |
| Enterprise | 25 | By quote | Unlimited | Unlimited |

On **Pro** and **Team** you buy seats one at a time and projects in packs of ten,
up to the plan's maximum; past that maximum the plan picker points you at
**Enterprise**. The **Seats** row then shows the split, for example
`7 (5 plan + 2 extra)`.

**Pro** does not include policies, reports, SBOM, AI-BOM, the EU AI Act report or
the Jira integration.

Lowering seats or projects is refused while more are in use than you are asking
for: remove the extra members or projects first.

If your organization is on an earlier plan, it keeps that plan's seats, projects
and limits, and its name carries **(previous offer)** on this page.

### Monthly Usage Limits

Your subscription includes per-seat limits for Cybe AI features:

- **Cybe Security Champion** — AI-powered vulnerability prioritization (per seat)
- **Cybe AutoFix** — Automated vulnerability remediation suggestions (per seat)
- **Cybe Analysis** — Deep code analysis and insights (per seat)

On **Pro**, **Team** and **Enterprise**, the organization's monthly Cybe allowance
is **300 credits per filled seat**: a seat you have paid for but not yet given to a
member adds nothing to the pool.

**Projects** are capped by your plan, as the table above shows. A project counts
against the cap the first time the AppSec Platform analyses it, and creating one
past the cap is refused — buy a pack of ten projects, or move up a plan.

<Tip>
A change that costs more — upgrading your plan, adding seats or projects, raising a credit pack, or switching to yearly billing — is invoiced for the rest of the current period as soon as you confirm it, and takes effect once that invoice is paid. If the payment needs authentication or your card is refused, you are sent to the invoice's payment page and nothing changes until it is paid. The full price then appears on your next invoice.

Lowering a quantity is not credited: what you give up stays available until the end
of the current period, and the lower amount is billed from the next one. Only one
change is applied at a time — if another change of your organization is still
going through, yours is refused and you can try again in a moment.
</Tip>

<Note>
Usage limits are **per seat**, but credits are pooled at the organization level. If one developer exceeds their allocation while another uses less, the organization's total remains balanced.
</Note>

### Promotion Codes

If you have a promotion code, enter it on the Stripe Checkout page that opens when you start a paid subscription.

The promotion code field is shown for **monthly** billing only. It does not appear on yearly billing, and add-on credit packs cannot be purchased with a code.

## Billing Information

Maintain accurate billing details to ensure proper invoicing and VAT compliance.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing/billing-information.png" alt="Billing Information" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

### General Information

- **Customer Type**: Individual or Company
- **First Name & Last Name**: Primary billing contact
- **Country**: Required for tax and VAT calculations, and it decides the currency you are billed in

### Billing Address

- **Address Line 1 & 2**: Complete street address
- **City & Postal Code**: Location details
- **Region/State**: If applicable

<Warning>
Ensure your billing information is accurate and up-to-date. This information appears on all invoices and is used for VAT/tax calculations.
</Warning>

### Billing Currency

Your billing country sets the currency: **USD** for the United States, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong and Japan, **EUR** for every other country. Until a billing country is set, prices follow the region your organization is hosted in: USD on the US deployment, EUR on the EU one.

The country you enter in the billing form when you upgrade applies immediately, to both the price you are quoted and the checkout itself. Set it before you confirm the upgrade if the pre-filled country is not the one you are billed from.

<Tip>
Click **Edit** in the top-right corner to update your billing information at any time.
</Tip>

## Manage Add-ons

Purchase additional credit packs to extend your Cybe AI capabilities beyond your base subscription limits.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing/manage-addons.png" alt="Manage Add-ons" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

### Available Add-on Packs

CybeDefend offers three types of credit packs:

1. **Cybe Security Champion Pack**  
   Extra Cybe Security Champion conversations  
   Pack size: 25 total for organization

2. **Cybe AutoFix Pack**  
   Extra Cybe AutoFix operations  
   Pack size: 25 total for organization

3. **Cybe Analysis Pack**  
   Extra Cybe Analysis operations  
   Pack size: 25 total for organization

### How to Purchase Add-ons

1. Click **Add** on the desired credit pack
2. Select the number of packs you need
3. Choose your billing cycle (Monthly or Yearly)
4. Review pricing and confirm

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/billing/addon-modal.png" alt="Add-on Purchase Modal" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

### Add-on Pricing

Add-ons are available with flexible billing options:

- **Monthly billing**: Pay per month for ongoing credit access
- **Yearly billing**: Save with annual pre-payment (typically includes discount)

<Info>
All add-on credits are pooled at the **organization level**. Any team member can use credits from any pack, regardless of who initiated the purchase.
</Info>

<Note>
Add-ons are prorated based on when you purchase them during your billing cycle: the prorated amount is invoiced when you confirm the purchase, and the credits are added once that invoice is paid. Removing packs is not credited: the packs stay available until the end of the current period, and the lower amount is billed from the next one. Unused credits from monthly packs expire at the end of each billing period.
</Note>
