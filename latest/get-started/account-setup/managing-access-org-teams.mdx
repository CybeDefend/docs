CybeDefend offers flexible structures to keep your organization and teams organized. Each **Organization** can have multiple **Teams**, and each Team can own projects (the codebase). This helps segment permissions and simplify collaboration.

<Tip>
Teams are ideal for grouping people working on the same projects or requiring similar levels of access.
</Tip>

## How to Create Teams

1. **Open Organization Settings**  
   From the main dashboard, select **Organization Settings**.
2. **Navigate to Teams**  
   Click **Teams** → **Create New Team**.
3. **Provide Basic Info**  
   Name your team and optionally include a description.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/get-started/setting-up-account/create-team-step.png" alt="Create Team Popup" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

<Note>
Each organization has at least **one** Team Manager, who can manage team members and their projects.
</Note>

## Inviting Organization Members

1. **Open the Invite Dialog**  
   - Go to **Organization Settings** → **+ Add Member**.
   - Enter the user’s email and assign an **Organization Role** (e.g., Administrator, Manager, Billing Manager, or Member).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/get-started/setting-up-account/invite-dialog.png" alt="Invite Organization Member Popup" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

The roles you can assign depend on your own:

| Your role | Roles you can invite |
|---|---|
| Creator | Administrator, Manager, Billing Manager, Member |
| Administrator | Manager, Billing Manager, Member |
| Manager | Manager, Member |

If you add the invitee to teams in the same dialog, you can only pick teams where you are allowed to add members yourself; otherwise the invitation is refused.

<Note>
The role is checked again when the invitee accepts. If your own role no longer allows it at that point — for example because you were removed from the organization — the invitation can no longer be accepted, and you need to send a new one.
</Note>

2. **Pending Invitations**  
   - Once invited, the user appears in the member list with a **Pending** status.
   - You can cancel the invite at any time if needed.
   - An invitation that gives the AppSec Platform holds an AppSec seat: it counts against your plan's seat allowance until it is accepted, cancelled or expired, and it is refused when no seat is free. An invitation that gives VibeDefend alone takes no AppSec seat. See [Seats and Invitations](/latest/plateform-overview/plan-&-pricing/manage-billing-subscription#seats-and-invitations).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/get-started/setting-up-account/invite-pending-list.png" alt="Pending Invitation List" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

3. **User Acceptance Flow**  
   - **Receive Email**: The invitee gets an email with a unique link.  
   - **Sign Up or Log In**: If they don’t have a CybeDefend account, they’ll be prompted to create one.  
   - **Accept Invitation**: After confirming, they’re redirected to the main dashboard with new **Organization Access**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/get-started/setting-up-account/invitation-landing-page.png" alt="Invitation Landing Page" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

<Note>
Admins can revoke an invitation before it’s accepted, ensuring you maintain full control over who joins your organization.
</Note>

### Per-member product access

An organization can run the **AppSec Platform**, **VibeDefend**, or both. Each
member holds the products you give them, and a member who opens a product they do
not hold is told to ask an administrator for it.

- Choose the products in the invite dialog. Name none and the invitation gives
  every active product you are allowed to give.
- You can only give a product that is **active** on the organization, and, as a
  manager, only a product you hold yourself.
- The creator and the administrators always hold both products: you cannot take one
  away from them, and promoting someone to administrator gives them both.
- Change a member's products at any time from the member list. An empty selection
  is allowed — the member stays in the organization and holds no product.

Over the API, the products are a field of the invitation, and a member's products
are set in one call:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api-eu.cybedefend.com/organization/$ORG_ID/invite" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"email":"dev@example.com","role":"member","products":["vibedefend"]}'

curl -X PUT "https://api-eu.cybedefend.com/organization/$ORG_ID/members/$USER_ID/products" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"products":["appsec","vibedefend"]}'
```

Use `https://api-us.cybedefend.com` if your organization is hosted on the US
deployment.

## Adding Team Members

1. **Go to Teams**  
   In **Organization Settings** → **Teams**, pick the team you want to modify.
2. **+ Add Member**  
   Enter the user’s email and a team role (Team Manager, Analyst Developer, Developer, or Read Only).
3. **Save & Notify**  
   Once they accept your organization invite, they’ll see team details and projects.
