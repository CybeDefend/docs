CybeDefend simplifies onboarding by allowing you to create an account through **OAuth2** (Google, Microsoft, GitHub, or GitLab). After signing in, you can form an **Organization** to group your projects and manage access. Once your repositories are connected, CybeDefend automatically starts scanning them.

---

## 2. Create Your Organization

1. **Access the Form**  
   After your first login, you’ll be prompted to create an **Organization**. Alternatively, visit **Organization Settings** → **New Organization**.
2. **Fill in Details**  
   Provide an organization name, an optional description, and (if needed) a logo.
3. **Save & Continue**  
   This sets up your top-level group where teams and projects will live.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/get-started/setting-up-account/organization-creation-step.png" alt="Create Organization Form" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem' }} />
</Frame>

<Note>
You can create multiple organizations if you manage different companies, clients, or environments.
</Note>

## 3. Choose Your Products

Once the organization exists, you are asked which products to turn on. Tick one or
both:

- **AppSec Platform** — scan your projects, then triage and fix their
  vulnerabilities with Cybe's AI analysis. It starts on a free trial of **10 scans
  and 50 Cybe credits**, no card needed; pick a plan when the trial runs out.
- **VibeDefend** — guardrails and security rules for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and
  your team's other coding agents. It is free every month up to a monthly
  allowance, no card needed.

<Note>
Only an organization administrator or a billing manager can turn a product on or
off. Turning one off keeps your data: turning it back on brings your projects,
results, rules and plan back as they were.
</Note>

Members then see the products you gave them — see
[Per-member product access](/latest/get-started/account-setup/managing-access-org-teams#per-member-product-access).

## 4. Connect Your Repositories

1. **Select Your Version Control Platform**  
   CybeDefend supports GitHub. Check out our [Detailed Integration Guides](/latest/get-started/connect-your-source-code/connect-github) for each platform.
2. **Authorize Access**  
   Approve read (and optionally write) permissions so we can scan your code.
3. **Pick Your Repositories**  
   Choose which repos to include in your **Organization**. You can assign them to specific teams or projects later.
4. **Instant Scanning**  
   As soon as you connect a repository, CybeDefend triggers an initial scan, identifying potential vulnerabilities.

<Tip>
Once your repositories are connected, scans start automatically—no extra steps required.
</Tip>

## Delete an Organization

Only the account that created an organization may delete it, and the deletion is
confirmed with a 6-digit code e-mailed to that account. The deletion is available
over the API.

<Warning>
Deleting an organization is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes its teams
and projects, their scans, findings, policies and stored files, and cancels the
organization's subscriptions. The creator keeps their own user account.
</Warning>

Ask for the code, then send it as `code` in the body of the delete call:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api-eu.cybedefend.com/organization/$ORG_ID/deletion-code" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
# 202 Accepted — {"expiresAt":"<ISO 8601 timestamp>"}

curl -X DELETE "https://api-eu.cybedefend.com/organization/$ORG_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"code":"012345"}'
```

Use `https://api-us.cybedefend.com` if your organization is hosted on the US
deployment.

A code is valid **10 minutes** and is used once; asking for another one replaces
it. A code may be submitted **5 times**, after which you ask for a new one. At
most **3 codes an hour** are sent, **60 seconds** apart.

A refusal carries its reason as `code` in the body. Switch on that value, never on
the message:

| `code` | HTTP | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `NOT_ORGANIZATION_CREATOR` | 403 | The caller did not create this organization |
| `DELETION_CODE_INVALID` | 400 | Wrong, expired, already used, missing or malformed |
| `DELETION_CODE_ATTEMPTS_EXCEEDED` | 400 | The code's 5 attempts are spent — ask for a new code |
| `DELETION_CODE_RATE_LIMITED` | 429 | Another code was asked for too soon, or too many this hour (`Retry-After: 60`) |
| `DELETION_LOCKED` | 429 | Ten wrong codes within 24 hours — the deletion is locked for 24 hours |

<Warning>
`DELETE /organization/{organizationId}` takes a body carrying the confirmation
code, and answers **400** without it:

```diff
- DELETE /organization/{organizationId}
+ POST   /organization/{organizationId}/deletion-code
+ DELETE /organization/{organizationId}   {"code":"012345"}
```

An integration that calls it as another member of the organization, or with no
body, is refused: the call has to be made by the creator's account, with a code
e-mailed to that account.
</Warning>
